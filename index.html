The project brief

A more useful first conversation starts before the survey.

Instead of a blank contact form, this concept gathers the practical context around your idea.

  1. 01

    Name the project

    Choose a driveway, patio, paving or garden-transformation route, then describe what you want to change.

  2. 02

    Share the shape of it

    Add an approximate size, a few reference images and any details that help explain the site.

  3. 03

    Set the survey preference

    Leave a preferred time to discuss a survey, with a budget range to frame the conversation.

Bring the useful details

A few simple inputs can make the next step feel much more considered.

  • 01 A rough width, length or garden size is enough to start.
  • 02 Inspiration can be a photo from your phone or a saved idea.
  • 03 A budget range is context for a conversation, not a price list.
  • 04 Choose a preferred survey window that works around your week.
